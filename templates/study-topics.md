# Template: Study Topics

> Structural template only. One list per candidate. A running list of
> concepts, tools, or techniques worth studying later that came up in
> passing — during a mock interview, its evaluation, or a corrections
> debrief — but weren't themselves the subject of the question being
> asked. Distinct from the
> [practice profile](practice-profile.md), which tracks scored
> performance against the question bank; this tracks the candidate's
> own "look into this later" list. Internal only, like the rest of the
> practice track — never sent to anyone.

```markdown
# Study Topics — <candidate name>

Status: active
Last updated: <date>
Internal only: personal study list, never sent to anyone.

## Open Topics

### <topic name>

- First surfaced: <date>
- Context: <the specific question, discussion, or session this came up
  in — link to the session artifact, and quote or summarize the
  exchange closely enough that re-reading this later actually explains
  why the topic came up, not just that it did>
- Why it matters: <how it connects to a target track, a real project,
  a question bank topic/ID, or a stated career direction>
- Status: open | explored (<date> — what was learned, or where notes
  on it live) | dropped (<date> — why>

## Explored / Resolved

<moved here once studied, with a short outcome note; kept, not
deleted, per rules/general.md>
```
