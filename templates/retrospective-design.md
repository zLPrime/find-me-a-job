# Template: Retrospective Design

> Structural template only. One artifact per concept, built by the
> [retrospective-design-agent](../agents/retrospective-design-agent.md)
> with the candidate. Everything outside "The real problem" is
> hypothetical and is never presented as something the project did.

```markdown
# Retrospective Design — <topic> — <C-nn> <concept> — <date>

Status: in progress | complete | abandoned
Last updated: <date and time>
Basis: hypothetical — not something the project did
Internal only: practice material, never sent to anyone.
Topic map: <link>
Concept mapping: <link to the concept's entry>
Project: <project name as the candidate profile states it>
Language: <language>

## The real problem

- Problem: <what happened, in the candidate's facts> — input/notes.md,
  <date>
- What was actually done: <facts only> — input/notes.md, <date>

## Transcript

### Candidate's approach

**Tutor:** <question>

**Candidate:** <answer in the candidate's own words>

**Tutor:** `[term: <term>]` <the moment the term was introduced, and
how>

### Filling the gaps

**Tutor:** <explanation of a part the candidate didn't reach, applied to
the problem>

**Candidate:** <their own restatement or application>

### Trade-offs

**Tutor:** <question>

**Candidate:** <answer>

## The design, in hindsight

- How <concept> would have applied: <applied to this problem, step by
  step, in plain language>
- Terms introduced: <term — one-line meaning, as applied here>

## Trade-offs

- Cost on that project: <time, complexity, people, migration>
- Worth it there? <yes / partly / no — and why>
- Why it wasn't done: <the candidate's honest reason>
- When the candidate would use it: <the conditions>

## Framing line

"<Looking back, … — agreed with the candidate>"

## New Facts and Study Topics

- New facts: <recorded in input/notes.md on <date>, or "none">
- Study topics: <recorded in study-topics.md, or "none">
```
