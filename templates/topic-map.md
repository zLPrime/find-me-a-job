# Template: Topic Map

> Structural template only — no real topic or candidate data.
> Illustrative examples must be clearly labeled per
> [examples/README.md](../examples/README.md). One map per topic per
> candidate, built by the
> [topic-map-agent](../agents/topic-map-agent.md). The map describes
> the topic, not a project, so every project framed in the topic
> shares it; everything after it is kept per project.

```markdown
# Topic Map — <topic, as the candidate named it>

Status: draft | confirmed | retired
Last updated: <date>
Prepared by: topic-map-agent
Internal only: practice material, never sent to anyone.
Purpose: <link to the vacancy artifact, a role type, or "general
  preparation">
Projects to frame: <project names exactly as the candidate profile
  states them>
Interview language: <language>
Change note: <what was added, retired, or reweighted in this version,
  and why — including the candidate's scope review>

## Concepts

| ID | Area | Concept | Weight | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | <area> | <concept> | high / medium / low | <standard coverage / vacancy requirement / prep-note signal / candidate request> | active / retired (<reason>) |

## Concept detail

### C-01 — <concept>

- Definition: <one or two plain-language lines; note any disagreement
  between schools of thought>
- Evidence signals (what a project shows when this was present):
  - <observable decision, structure, conversation, or problem>
- Counter-evidence (what shows it was absent, or a look-alike that
  isn't it):
  - <observable sign>
- Extraction prompts (no topic vocabulary; each answerable with "no"):
  - P1: <question about the project's facts>
    - Also touches: <other concept IDs, or "none">
  - P2: <question>

## Open Questions

- <anything uncertain about the topic's scope or the purpose>
```
